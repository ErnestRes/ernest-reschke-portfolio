About Me

Ernest Reschke

Computer Engineering & Business · Kettering University

I am drawn to environments that push me to improve. Whether in engineering, business, or personal experiences, I consistently choose the harder path because it forces growth. Over time I've built a mindset centered around adaptability, persistence, and continuous improvement — one that shapes how I approach both education and professional work.

My career goals didn't come from watching my dad live them out, but from the stories he shared. Through his 23-year military career, he showed me what success looks like — not just financially, but in terms of freedom, impact, and building a meaningful life. That foundation motivates me to pursue opportunities that challenge me and allow me to create real impact through my work.

Engineering Background

My interest in engineering began in high school when I took apart a computer and became fascinated by how hardware and software interact. That curiosity led me to Computer Engineering at Kettering, where I've worked across embedded systems, automated testing, and lean engineering — from building a Spotify playback controller on an ESP32, to developing a Python-based automated testing toolchain at Detroit Diesel that reduced engineers' testing time by 20%.
